# Buildings

All building geometry is generated by code in this repository (MIT licence). No floorplans,
models, textures or pictures from third parties are included or traced; the generators follow
general design rules only. Reference images people look at for inspiration are not copied.

| generator | module | content | used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| single rooms | `room.py` | rectangular room with one door, wall thickness, boxes | E0/E1 (frozen) |
| apartments | `apartment.py` | hallway with rooms on both sides, living area, closed doors, pass-through, wardrobes | apartment study, sim2 |
| houses | `house.py` | 8-12 rooms on a long hallway or an entry hall with cross corridor, open-plan openings | sim2 |
| residences | `residence.py` | single-family houses by zones (below) | sim2 |
| training houses | `trainhouse.py` | generic modular layout (below) | sim2 |

## Residences (`residence.py`)

- **Zones:** a private wing (bedrooms on a corridor; master bedroom with en-suite bathroom and
  walk-in closet), a core (entrance hall, hallway, guest WC) and a public wing (living room,
  dining room and kitchen joined by wide openings, utility room). Most houses have a garage next
  to the utility room (L-shaped footprint). Layouts are mirrored at random.
- **Windows** in exterior walls of habitable rooms; they block bodies, not sight.
- **Typed furniture** per room type with a height: beds, sofas, tables, desks, counters, kitchen
  islands and baths are *low*, wardrobes, shelves, fridges and cars are *tall*.
- **Guarantee:** every room is reachable for a body from the entrance; a layout that fails is
  drawn again from a derived seed, so a seed always gives the same house.

## Furniture height and sight (`geom.SIGHT_H`)

Furniture lower than 1.2 m blocks bodies but not sight: a standing observer (eyes at about
1.6 m) still sees the upper body of a standing person behind a sofa, table or counter. Taller
pieces block both. This is a modelling choice; crouching people and partial cover are not
modelled. Earlier generators (apartments, houses) treat all furniture as tall and are unchanged.

## Training houses (`trainhouse.py`)

- **Module grid:** square modules of 2.8-3.2 m in 4-6 columns and 3-4 rows, one or two corridor
  bands of 1.4 m between the rows and sometimes a corridor column; neighbouring modules are merged
  into larger rooms at random (7-21 rooms).
- **Links:** a spanning tree from the entrance with open doorways or doors (never a door opening
  into a corridor), plus extra links between rooms for loops.
- **Furniture:** sparse low barriers and tall partitions.
- **Viewer:** plywood-style walls and a see-through observer catwalk on top of the walls, drawn
  only (`"style": "trainhouse"` in the geometry), not part of the simulation.
- A generic type in the spirit of reconfigurable training buildings; it does not reproduce any
  real facility.

## Viewer look

Furniture is drawn with simple shapes built from boxes and cylinders in `viewer3d.html`: beds with
headboard and pillow, sofas with backrest, tables and desks on legs, kitchen counters with worktop
and splashback, wardrobes, shelves, a fridge, baths, cars with bonnet, glass band, roof and
wheels, barriers and partitions. Each piece fills exactly its simulated footprint and height;
backs face the nearest wall. Floors follow the room type (wood planks, tiles, concrete). All
textures are drawn by code at load time. There are no external models, textures or fonts in the
repository.
